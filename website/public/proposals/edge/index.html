Edge Platform Family proposals ​

The Edge Platform Family, edge.forms.takoform.com/v1beta1, is the first official Form Family (decision 0009). Its members fix, completely, the application-visible semantics of a proven edge developer platform without naming its vendor (decision 0008).

Authoring policy: shape-preserving contracts ​

Every member Form preserves one service shape end to end: execution ABI, client API, data model, consistency, delivery guarantees, update and delete units, error semantics, and the capabilities exposed through typed Bindings. No free semantic token is admitted; a difference in semantics is a different Form, never a selector value. Outward capability use is a digest-bound Binding held by a revision resource; inward activation is an attachment resource (decision 0010). Desired schemas carry no name or envelope plumbing: the v1beta1 resource envelope owns identity and status (decision 0011).

The catalog source of truth is internal/edgeformcatalog; the generated candidates live in forms/candidates/edge/v1beta1, the exact Interface and Binding candidates in interfaces/candidates/v1alpha1 and bindings/candidates/v1alpha1.

Designs that differ in these semantics — PostgresDatabase, FifoQueue, WasiFunction, ContainerService, TimezoneSchedule — belong to future families and get their own proposals when that work starts, per spec/form-families.md.
